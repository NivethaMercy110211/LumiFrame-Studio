Established 2011

15 Years of Fine Art Heritage

Founded on the principle that portraiture should be treated as fine art, not fast digital transactions.

LumiFrame Studio was born in San Francisco with a single mission: to provide families and individuals with heirloom portraits that grow more precious with every passing decade. Over 15 years, we have welcomed over 3,000 families through our doors.

Artistic Vision

Our Creative Philosophy

"We don't take photographs; we sculpt light around genuine emotion to capture the soul of the moment."

We reject rigid awkward posing and unnatural filter presets. Instead, we use custom light shaping to flatter natural skin tones while building an atmosphere where our subjects feel confident and relaxed.

Mastery of Craft

Our Photography Approach

Precision optics meet empathetic human storytelling.

  • Individualized Lighting Architecture: Tailored light placement for every skin tone and face structure.
  • Guided Posing Direction: No standing awkwardly; we guide your eyes, hands, and posture naturally.
  • Hand Retouching: Non-destructive texture retouching that maintains authentic skin pores and character.
